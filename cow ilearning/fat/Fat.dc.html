Fat is a fibre story

If the fat in your milk is low, look at the fodder before the grain bag.
F1 Farm One editors6 min read · reviewed by [Vet reviewer name]

A cow’s rumen is a fermentation tank. Fibre makes her chew, chewing makes saliva, and saliva keeps the tank from turning sour. A sour rumen makes less milk fat.

That is why the guide begins with the 60:40 rule: roughly 60 parts forage to 40 parts concentrate, with fodder chopped to about 1.5 to 2.5 cm.

Keep the rumen above 6.0

Even a good ration can turn acid on a heavy-grain day. The guide adds sodium bicarbonate at 0.75 to 1.0% of dry matter intake plus yeast culture to hold rumen pH above 6.0.
