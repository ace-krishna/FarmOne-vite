WATER MORE MILK

Water is the cheapest feed you are not measuring

Milk is mostly water. A cow that cannot drink enough cannot make enough.
F1 Farm One editors5 min read · reviewed by [Vet reviewer name]

A litre of milk is close to nine-tenths water. Where does the cow get it? From the trough, mostly.

The guide puts the need at 4 to 5 litres of water for every litre of milk. A cow giving 12 litres a day should be drinking something like 48 to 60 litres. Most farmers never check.

“A trough that is too far away is a feed shortage in disguise.”

Where to put the trough

The guide recommends troughs within 15 metres of the cows and cleaned every day. Stale, dirty water cuts drinking, and drinking drives how much dry feed a cow can eat.

KEY TAKEAWAYS
  • Plan for 4 to 5 litres of water per litre of milk.
  • Keep troughs within 15 m of the cows.
  • Clean troughs daily so cows keep drinking.
