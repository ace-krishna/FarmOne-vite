DEAD BIRDS DISEASE CONTROL

Dead birds: the 24-hour rule

Some birds will die on any farm. What you do in the next day decides whether it stays a small loss.

A dead bird left in the shed spreads disease and draws flies, rats and stray dogs. It also smells.

So the rule is simple. Take dead birds away from the live ones right away, keep them in a closed container, and dispose of them within 24 hours.

“Fewer birds die when you look after them well. Prevention is the first step.”

What a burial pit looks like

A good pit is deep, narrow and covered. The lid keeps flies and rats out.

SEE IT · BURIAL PIT
Pit width: 0.8 to 1.2 m. When it is full, cover it with 0.5 m of packed soil, heaped above ground. Keep pits at least 1 m apart.
KEY TAKEAWAYS
  • Remove dead birds at once and dispose of them within 24 hours.
  • Keep them in a closed container until then.
  • Bury them deep and covered, or compost them in a roofed, fenced bay.
  • Used vaccine vials and syringes follow the solid-waste rules, not the carcass pit.
