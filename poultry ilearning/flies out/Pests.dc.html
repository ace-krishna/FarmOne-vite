Keep flies and rats out, and medicine in its place

Pests are easier to prevent than to chase. So is the misuse of medicine.

Flies breed in wet manure, spilled feed and rotting waste. Rats come for the feed and the shelter. Both carry disease, and both are a sign that something in the shed is untidy.

The best fix is to take away what they come for.

Medicine is not a feed additive

Some farmers mix antibiotics into feed to make birds grow faster. Indian feed standards say not to. Tap each card to check what you know.

MYTH OR FACT?
KEY TAKEAWAYS
  • Remove what flies and rats come for: wet manure, spilled feed, dead birds.
  • Lock rats out first, then trap.
  • Keep the feed store raised, concrete and clean.
  • Use antibiotics only for sick birds, and only on a vet’s prescription.
