Poultry across India: where the birds are

India keeps more than 850 million poultry birds. See how your state stacks up.
F1 Farm One editors4 min read · 20th Livestock Census figures

Poultry farming in India has grown into a large, lively industry. The 20th Livestock Census counts 851.8 million birds across the country.

The leading states are Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana and West Bengal, followed by Maharashtra, Karnataka, Assam, Haryana, Kerala and Odisha.
