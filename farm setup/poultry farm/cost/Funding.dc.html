The subsidy for rural poultry

The National Livestock Mission pays 50% of the cost, up to ₹25 lakh. It is for breeding units that supply chicks to villages, not for ordinary broiler sheds. Check each point that is true for you.

KEY TAKEAWAYS
  • A 10,000-broiler unit costs about ₹31.8 lakh in all. A 20,000-layer unit costs about ₹1.11 crore.
  • Banks usually want about a quarter of the cost from you as margin money.
  • Feed is the biggest cost, so small price changes decide your profit.
  • NLM pays 50% up to ₹25 lakh, but only for rural breeding units.
