The feed store, at a glance

Feed grinding makes dust, and spilled feed brings pests. The feed store sits near the farm gate, apart from the poultry sheds.

SEE IT · FEED STORE LAYOUT
KEY TAKEAWAYS
  • Store manure under cover, on a hard base, 2 m above the water table.
  • Raise the feed store floor 2 ft, fit a dust collector, and keep a tyre dip at the gate.
  • Collect wash water in a tank and use it on your green belt.
  • Have a burial pit, compost bay or incinerator ready before you stock birds.
