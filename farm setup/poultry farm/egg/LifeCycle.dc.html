KEY TAKEAWAYS
  • Hatching takes about 21 days. Broilers go to market at 40 to 45 days.
  • Layers move through three houses, and lay from 18 weeks to about 75 weeks.
  • Most broilers live on deep litter. Most layers live in cages.
  • Pick your link in the chain before you plan the shed.
