What “consent” means

Farms with more than 25,000 birds at one place need two permissions from the State Pollution Control Board, under the Water Act 1974 and the Air Act 1981.

Good news: poultry farms are in the “Green” category of industries, so consent stays valid for 15 years.

Farms that share

Several farms close together can share one manure facility, either composting, biogas, or both. If you are in a cluster, this can save each farm money.

KEY TAKEAWAYS
  • Small: 5,000 to 25,000 birds. Medium: up to 1 lakh. Large: above 1 lakh.
  • Above 25,000 birds, get consent to establish and consent to operate.
  • Consent for poultry farms lasts 15 years.
  • Small farms compost manure. Bigger farms can also use biogas.
