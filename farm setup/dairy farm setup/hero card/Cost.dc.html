“Stagger your purchases: buy 5 cows first and 5 more after 5 to 6 months.”

That is the guide’s own procurement tip. Cows go dry at different times, and two rounds keep milk, and cash, flowing all year.

Lay out the shed

Each adult cow needs 40 to 50 sq. ft. under cover. A 40 × 15 ft shed (600 sq. ft.) fits ten in either arrangement. Run its long side east to west so afternoon sun stays out.
