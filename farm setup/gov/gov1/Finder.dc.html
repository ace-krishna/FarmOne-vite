Two kinds of help

Money you do not repay. This is a capital subsidy. The government pays part of the cost, for example 50% of a new set of milch animals.

A cheaper loan. This is interest help. You still repay the bank, but at a lower rate. The Kisan Credit Card and the AHIDF scheme work this way.

Who are you, in short?

A few shortcuts for the most common farmers.

SMALL FARMER, 2 TO 6 ANIMALS
Start with MahaBMS for the animals, and the Kisan Credit Card for feed and medicine money.
YOUNG ENTREPRENEUR, 18 TO 45, 10 TO 20 ANIMALS
Look at the Mukhyamantri Dairy Entrepreneurship scheme for interest help on your loan.
PLANNING TO PROCESS OR CHILL MILK
AHIDF is built for you: processing, value-added products, bulk coolers and feed plants.
KEY TAKEAWAYS
  • Choose by what you want to do this year, not by scheme name.
  • SC/ST farmers get a higher subsidy on MahaBMS (75%) and NABARD (33.33%).
  • Some schemes pay you; some make your loan cheaper.
  • Central and Maharashtra schemes can be used for different parts of your farm.
