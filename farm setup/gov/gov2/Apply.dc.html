What goes in your project report?

The report is called a DPR. It is simply your farm plan on paper. It should say:

How many animals (for example, 10 cows)
The shed design
Machinery and its cost
Your fodder plan and the money you expect
KEY TAKEAWAYS
  • Plan, papers, register, wait, buy, then receive the subsidy.
  • Do not buy before the Pre-Sanction Order.
  • An officer will inspect and geo-tag what you bought.
  • The subsidy goes to your Aadhaar-linked bank account.
